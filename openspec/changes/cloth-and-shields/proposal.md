# Proposal

## Why

Reviewing the redesigned warriors in the unit lab showed two problems:
- **The fist goes through the shield.** Shields were mounted at the elbow (the forearm bone's origin), with only 7 cm of offset.
- **The cape looks primitive.** It's a short, rigid sheet glued to the back, which sticks out like a board whenever the body bends.

## What Changes

- **Shields are strapped to the forearm,** as the shield clips expect. The centre sits two-thirds of the way to the wrist and the face points out of the back of the hand, clear of a heavy arm, so the fist stays behind it in every clip.
- **Capes are simulated cloth.** They're generated by the client, not baked:
  - a knee-length grid, narrow at the shoulders and wider at the hem, with a darker hem and collar;
  - hung from the spine bone, with gravity, air drag, and distance constraints for structure, shear and bending;
  - colliding with capsules around the torso and legs (sized for the build), so they drape over the back and trail behind a running unit;
  - reset on teleports, and settled over the pose for still renders (preview, portraits).

  In the unit lab's walk test a headwind stands in for movement.
- Cloth items are declared in `parts.json` (`kind: cloth`, width, length, team colour). The rigid cape mesh is removed.

## Out of scope

- Cloth for other garments (robes, banners), and wind in battles.
- Self-collision of the cloth, and collision with shields and weapons.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
None.

### Modified Capabilities
- `unit-looks`: adds that shields are strapped to the forearm clear of the hand, and that capes are simulated cloth.

## Impact

- `tools/blender/bake_parts.py` (shield poses, cloth items, no rigid cape);
- `web/public/assets/parts/` (manifest, Cape files removed);
- new `web/src/scene/cloth.ts`;
- `scene/models.ts` (cloth items, `tick` and `settle` on characters);
- `Units.tsx`, `UnitLab.tsx`, `UnitPreview.tsx` and `portraits.ts` (tick or settle the cloth).
