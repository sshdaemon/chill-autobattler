# Huslyar

## Concept
The court singer of the Kyivan Rus druzhyna: an older man (or woman) in a long
belted caftan with a husli, the winged lap harp, on a strap. He sings the old
glories of the prince's men at feasts and on the march, like Boyan, the singer
of "earlier times" in *The Tale of Igor's Campaign*. He carries only a belt
knife.

## Role on the battlefield
Support. He's weak on his own on purpose: five huslyars lose to anything, and
he never wins a fight alone. Inside an army he makes the units around him
stronger. His song (a trait) gives allies within 6 m more damage, and his
learnable songs add attack speed, toughness, a rally when a comrade falls, a
mocking song that slows the enemy's blows, or a longer song reach.

- Stays about 3 m behind his front line (stays near melee), keeps 5 m from
  enemies and falls back below 50% health. He only uses the knife when he's
  cornered.
- No matchup bonus. He doesn't beat anything; armies that keep him alive and
  close to their melee do.
- What beats him: anything that reaches the back line (charges, ranged units
  that pick off specials, fear), and armies that split up so his song reaches
  few allies.

## How it should feel
Calm, grey-bearded, unarmoured. Undyed linen and ochre or brown wool, a
silvered belt, a cap or bare head (no wizard hat). The husli is the
silhouette: a flat wooden wing held across the chest. When he sings, a soft
warm ripple shows the song's reach. Never magic: no glows, bolts or sparkles.

## Skill ideas
1. **Song of glory:** allies within the song attack faster.
2. **Tale of the bohatyrs:** allies within the song take less damage.
3. **Lament for the fallen:** when an ally dies within the song, the allies
   there hit harder for a few seconds.
4. **Mocking song:** enemies near him attack more slowly.
5. **Loud voice:** the song reaches further.

## Avoid
- Fantasy-bard clichés: lutes, feathered hats, spell effects, fireballs.
- Later Muscovite or Russian imagery (kokoshnik, onion domes, balalaika,
  gusli of the 19th-century "boyar" style) and Cossack kobza or bandura players.
- Making him a damage dealer: his knife is there so a lone huslyar can finish
  a battle, nothing more.
