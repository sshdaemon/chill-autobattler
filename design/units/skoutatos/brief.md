# Skoutatos

## Concept
The heavy infantryman of the Roman (Byzantine) army of the 10th–11th century,
drilled by the book: the Taktika of Leo VI and the *Praecepta militaria* of
Nikephoros II. He stands in the front ranks behind a long shield (*skoutarion*,
the source of his name) with a spear (*kontarion*) and a sword at his side.

## Role on the battlefield
The wall: slow, tough, holds the line. It waits in formation while the enemy
closes, then advances with its neighbours; it never falls back. It is at its
best beside other skoutatoi and facing the enemy.

- Beats: ranged units that come within reach (×1.35 against ranged), and melee
  that attacks it head-on (Shield wall), above all a charge into a line of
  several skoutatoi (Phalanx).
- Beaten by: fire that punishes a packed line (siphonarios), hard-hitting
  melee that gets in among it (huskarl), and ranged units kept out of reach.

## How it should feel
Heavy, steady, disciplined. Slower than every other foot soldier, with a
longer reach than a swordsman. Silhouette: a tall oval shield on the left arm
covering shoulder to knee, a spear held level, a simple rounded iron helmet.
Colours from `design/SETTING.md`: red and white on the shield (a cross or
Chi-Rho), dark-blue or green padded coats, iron and bronze. No purple, no gold,
nothing Crusader.

## Skill ideas
1. **Shield wall**: much less damage from blows that come from the front.
2. **Phalanx**: less damage taken for each other skoutatos close by.
3. **Kontarion**: a longer spear, more reach.
4. **Drill**: attacks faster (the manuals' drilled thrusts).
5. **Klibanion**: lamellar armour, more health.

## Avoid
- Purple or gold on soldiers, Crusader kit (great helms, surcoats, kite
  shields with heraldry), Roman legionary kit of the 1st century (that
  *scutum* and *galea* are a thousand years earlier).
- Charging off alone: it waits and keeps to its line.
