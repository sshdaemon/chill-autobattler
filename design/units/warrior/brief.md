## Concept
The army's front line: trained soldiers who close in fast and hit hard.
<!-- e.g. "veteran sellswords", "heavy knights", "shield-wall infantry",
"barbarian raiders"? -->

## Role on the battlefield (fixed, for reference)
Melee, 100 HP, 10–16 damage every 1.3 s, 1.5 m reach, 3.2 m/s. Strong against
archers (×1.45), slightly weak against mages (×0.95). Skills: Toughness,
Swift, Strength, Dash (charge), Executioner (finisher).

## How it should feel
Heavy and dependable: broad shoulders, a big weapon, armour that reads from the
battle camera. Should stand out from archers and mages by silhouette alone.
