# Strilets

**Scope:** redesign, balance and dress the placeholder strilets (unit id 4,
Kyivan Rus ranged). Its id is fixed. Its numbers start from
`design/ROSTER-PLAN.md`; its look follows `design/SETTING.md`.

## Concept
The archer of the prince's druzhyna, or of a town levy called out for the
season. He shoots a short composite recurved bow of steppe type, the kind found
in Rus burials at Shestovytsia and Chernihiv, and learned to fight like the
steppe riders he meets on the southern border: never stand still, shoot while
giving ground.

## Role on the battlefield
Mobile ranged skirmisher. He keeps about 7 m between himself and whoever comes
at him and backs off in short bursts; with Steppe shooting he keeps loosing
arrows while he does it. In the Rus army he stands behind the druzhynnyky,
who guard him, and next to the huslyar.

- **Beats:** slow melee that cannot catch him (warrior, huskarl, skoutatos),
  and casters through range (mage, völva).
- **Beaten by:** other ranged units with longer reach or more health (archer
  duels are close to even), fast gap-closers.

## How it should feel
Light and quick, never cornered. Distinct from the generic archer by
silhouette: a short, strongly curved bow instead of a long stave, a
fur-trimmed cap instead of a hood, the quiver on the belt instead of the back.

- **Colours:** undyed linen and brown wool, ochre trim, dark horn-and-sinew bow,
  a little team colour on the coat.
- **Clothes:** padded or leather coat, belted; trousers into soft boots.

## Skill ideas
1. **Long shot:** more range.
2. **Steppe shooting:** keeps shooting while backing away (no pause to aim);
   the signature skill.
3. **Bodkin points:** armour-piercing heads, more damage against melee.
4. **Arcing shot:** shoots over rocks and trees.
5. **Quick draw:** faster shooting.

## Avoid
- 16th-century streltsy: no long red caftans, berdysh axes or muskets. The
  name is the Ukrainian word for archer, nothing more.
- Cossack imagery, onion domes, kokoshnik headdresses.
- Longbows and fantasy bows: the bow is short, recurved and dark.
- The word "Russian" anywhere; it is Rus or Kyivan Rus.
