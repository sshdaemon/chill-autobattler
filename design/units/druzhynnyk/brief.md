# Druzhynnyk

## Concept
The prince's retainer: a professional warrior of the Kyivan Rus druzhyna
(10th–11th century) who lives, eats and rides with the prince. Mail shirt,
conical helmet with a mail neck guard, crimson round shield, and a sword, a small
battle axe or a spear.

## Role on the battlefield
Balanced, armoured front line that protects the archers (strilets) and the
singer (huslyar) instead of running off after kills. It advances in line with
its allies, stays within 8 m of them, and goes for the enemy closest to its own
ranged and special units.

- Beats: ranged units that reach it (×1.4 against ranged); chargers that dash
  into it (Braced spear).
- Beaten by: hard-hitting melee (huskarl), massed melee caught by fire
  (siphonarios), and anything that simply kites it, since it won't chase far.

## How it should feel
Steady, well-armoured, together. Not the fastest or the hardest hitter, but it
holds and doesn't leave its archers. Medium-heavy silhouette: conical helmet,
crimson shield, mail, undyed linen and brown wool, silvered belt fittings.

## Skill ideas
1. **Shield wall**: less damage from arrows and bolts.
2. **Prince's guard**: takes part of the blows aimed at nearby archers and the
   singer.
3. **Braced spear**: an enemy that dashes at it is stopped short and takes a
   counter-blow.
4. **Heavy mail**: more health, a little slower.
5. **Axe and sword**: attacks faster.

## Avoid
- Great helms, plate, horned helmets, giant weapons (see `design/SETTING.md`).
- Later Muscovite or Cossack imagery: no bardiches, onion domes, double-headed
  eagles.
- "Russia" or "Russian": it is Rus or Kyivan Rus.
- Chasing kills across the map: it guards.
