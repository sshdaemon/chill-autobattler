# Toxotes

**Scope:** redesign, balance and dress the placeholder toxotes (unit id 10,
Roman ranged). Its id is fixed. Its numbers start from
`design/ROSTER-PLAN.md`; its look follows `design/SETTING.md`.

## Concept
The archer of a Roman *tagma*, drilled by the book: the Taktika of Leo VI and
the *Praecepta militaria* want archers in ranks behind the skoutatoi, shooting
on command at what the line is fighting. He carries a composite bow in a bow
case and a quiver, and some carry the *solenarion*, a guide tube the manuals
describe for shooting short darts further than a bow alone.

## Role on the battlefield
Disciplined line archer. Where the strilets gives ground and the bowman hunts,
the toxotai pick the target their comrades are already shooting, as long as it
is within their reach, and bring it down together. In the Roman army they stand
behind the skoutatoi, which keep melee off them.

- **Beats:** melee that walks into concentrated fire (warrior, druzhynnyk,
  skoutatos) more often than other archers do, and casters through range.
- **Beaten by:** fast melee that reaches the line (huskarl), and archers with
  more mobility in an open duel (close to even).

## How it should feel
Orderly and steady rather than nimble: a uniformed look, the same colours on
every man, arrows arriving on the same target. Silhouette: a short composite
bow, a quiver on the back, a round felt cap instead of a hood or fur cap.

- **Colours:** off-white and madder-red wool, undyed linen, brown leather;
  team colour on the coat. No purple, no gold.
- **Clothes:** padded coat (*kavadion*) to the knee, belted; trousers into
  boots.

## Skill ideas
1. **Solenarion:** shoots darts through the guide tube: much more range, a
   little less damage per dart.
2. **Volley:** more damage when two or more allies shoot the same target; the
   signature skill.
3. **Sharp arrows:** more damage.
4. **Arcing shot:** shoots over rocks and trees.
5. **Quick draw:** faster shooting.

## Avoid
- Purple and gold, Crusader kit (the Crusades start in 1096), "Roman legion"
  imagery from a thousand years earlier: no lorica segmentata, no red plumes.
- Longbows; the bow is short and composite.
- Fur caps and hoods (the strilets and the archer have those).
- "Byzantine" in unit text; they are Romans.
