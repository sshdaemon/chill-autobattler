# Huskarl

## Concept
A sworn household warrior of a Norse lord on the eastern road (late 10th–11th
century): the hired steel behind Volodymyr and Yaroslav, and the men who went
on to the emperor's Varangian Guard. His weapon is the Dane axe, a long
two-handed axe with a thin crescent blade; his painted round shield hangs on
his back while he swings it.

## Role on the battlefield
Shock troops: big blows, less armour. It goes for the enemy at once, never
falls back, and finishes whoever is already bleeding near it.

- Beats: ranged units it reaches (×1.45 against ranged); clumped units (the
  axe's sweep); anything already wounded (it picks the weakest within 4 m and
  executes it).
- Beaten by: armoured lines that meet the charge (druzhynnyk's braced spear,
  skoutatos), fire and fear that punish a reckless rush (siphonarios, völva),
  and kiters that run it around before it lands the first blow.

## How it should feel
Hard-hitting and reckless. Slower blows than a swordsman but each one hurts,
and a wide swing that catches a second man. Tall, open silhouette: the long
axe held across the body, shield slung on the back, rounded iron helmet with a
spectacle guard (the Gjermundbu find), blue or undyed wool, a mail-less tunic
for most. Shield paint in the Gokstad colours: yellow and black.

## Skill ideas
1. **Dane axe sweep**: blows also hit enemies right next to the target.
2. **Charge**: rushes at an enemy a short distance away.
3. **Battle fury**: hits harder when badly wounded.
4. **Shield-breaker**: its blows leave the target taking more damage for a while.
5. **Executioner**: may kill a badly wounded target outright.

## Avoid
- Horned or winged helmets, great helms, plate, fur-and-skulls "barbarian" kit
  (see `design/SETTING.md`).
- Giant fantasy axes or double-bladed axes: a Dane axe is long but light, with
  one thin blade.
- Later or modern Norse-revival imagery (runic tattoos, Valknut emblems).
- Hanging back: it charges at once and never retreats.
