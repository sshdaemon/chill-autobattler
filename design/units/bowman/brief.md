# Bowman

**Scope:** redesign, balance and dress the placeholder bowman (unit id 7,
Varangian ranged). Its id is fixed. Its numbers start from
`design/ROSTER-PLAN.md`; its look follows `design/SETTING.md`.

## Concept
A hunter-warrior of the eastern road: a man (or woman) who fed a longship's
crew with a bow in the forests along the Dnipro and fights with the same kit.
A long self bow cut from one stave of yew, like the bow found in the harbour
at Hedeby, and a couple of light throwing spears slung on the back for when
the prey comes close.

## Role on the battlefield
Aggressive ranged hunter. Unlike the strilets, who gives ground and keeps his
distance, the bowman walks forward to about 6 m of his quarry, picks the
enemy's archers and specials first, and finishes them with a thrown spear.
Light kit, little armour: reckless, hits hard, dies fast.

- **Beats:** archers and casters it can reach and outgun (mage, völva,
  strilets, toxotes) — a slight edge, not a hard counter.
- **Beaten by:** melee that reaches it (warrior, druzhynnyk); it does not
  kite away like the strilets.

## How it should feel
Bold and close-in for an archer. Silhouette: a tall, straight, pale bow
nearly as long as he is, a bundle of spears over the shoulder, bare head or
a simple wool hood; no armour.

- **Colours:** undyed and blue wool, brown leather, pale yew, iron spear
  heads; a little team colour on the tunic.
- **Clothes:** wool tunic, belt, trousers with leg wraps, turnshoes or soft
  boots.

## Skill ideas
1. **Throwing spears:** every so often a thrown spear for heavy damage; the
   signature skill.
2. **Hunter:** more damage against ranged and special units.
3. **Yew longbow:** more range.
4. **Arcing shot:** shoots over rocks and trees.
5. **Sea-hardened:** more health.

## Avoid
- Horned helmets, winged helmets, fur-and-horns "barbarian" imagery.
- Fantasy or English war-bow imagery: the bow is a plain D-section self bow,
  no recurve, no ornaments.
- Composite or recurved bows (that is the strilets's look).
